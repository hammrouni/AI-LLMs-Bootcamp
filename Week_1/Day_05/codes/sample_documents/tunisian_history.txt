Tunisian History and Carthaginian Civilization

Tunisia is the home of the great Carthage civilization that ruled the Mediterranean for centuries.
Carthage was a flourishing trading city led by the general Hannibal and his father Hamilcar.

Important Archaeological Sites:
1. Casbah of Tunis: The old capital surrounded by walls
2. Carthage: A city of Roman and Punic ruins
3. Kairouan: The capital of the Islamic Maghreb
4. Sousse: A city of fortresses and coastal walls
5. Dougga: An important archaeological site

Tunisian Cultural Heritage:
- Islamic art and architecture
- Traditional crafts: textiles, leather, pottery
- Famous Tunisian food: couscous, pasta, brik
- Andalusian music and Tunisian malouf
- Traditional dress and folk clothing
