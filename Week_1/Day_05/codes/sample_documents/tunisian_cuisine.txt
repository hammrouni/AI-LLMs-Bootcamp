Tunisian Cuisine: A True Taste of Mediterranean Civilization

Tunisian food reflects the country's rich history and diverse cultural influences.
From the Mediterranean Sea to the Sahara desert, the variety of Tunisian dishes is endless.

Main Dishes:
- Couscous: The national dish with dates and vegetables
- Grilled meats: Lamb grilled with traditional spices
- Brik: Both sweet and savory with cheese and fillings
- Rfissa: The finest type of dough with various fillings
- Malsouka: A popular dish made from green leaves

Appetizers and Desserts:
- Tunisian salad: Tomatoes, cucumbers, and onions
- Black olives from Tozeur and Gafsa
- Tunisian dates: The world's best date varieties
- Makroudh: Traditional sweets scented with orange blossom
- Deglet and Makherqa: Holiday pastries

Famous Tunisian Names:
- Ahmed and Fatima: Among the most popular names
- Noor Eddine and Zahra: Distinctive Tunisian names
- Khaled and Aisha: Classic Tunisian names
- Moukhtar and Saadia: Popular folk names
