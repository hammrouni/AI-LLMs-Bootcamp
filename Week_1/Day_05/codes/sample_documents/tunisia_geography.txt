Tunisia: The Pearl of North Africa

Tunisia is a country in North Africa with a rich history spanning thousands of years.

Geography:
- Bordered by Algeria and Libya
- Mediterranean coastline
- Sahara desert in the south
- Capital: Tunis
- Population: Over 12 million people

History:
- Ancient Carthage empire
- Roman colonization
- Islamic Golden Age
- Ottoman period
- French protectorate
- Independence in 1956

Culture:
- Arabic language and French influence
- Islamic traditions and customs
- Mediterranean heritage
- Strong family values
- Hospitality and warmth
